01 • Passive Infrastructure & Core Switching

Structured Cabling & High-Density Optical Fiber Backbones

A reliable corporate network starts with impeccable physical layer engineering. We design and install high-density structured cabling systems utilizing Low Smoke Zero Halogen (LSZH) Cat6, Cat6A, and Cat7 copper lines alongside OM3, OM4, and OS2 single-mode optical fiber backbones, paired with 10G/40G/100G leaf-spine campus switching.

In Simple Terms — Why It Matters

Over 70% of mysterious network slowdowns and random disconnects come from poorly crimped cables or cheap copper. We install fire-safe, neatly labeled cables and certify every single wall port with a Fluke DSX-8000 analyzer so your wiring is guaranteed for 25 years.

  • Every drop certified with Fluke DSX-8000 CableAnalyzer (PDF report provided)
  • Fusion optical splicing with < 0.02 dB insertion loss across floor risers
  • 25-Year Manufacturer System Performance Warranty
  • Compliant with ANSI/TIA-568.2-D and ISO/IEC 11801 international standards

05 • Critical Power, Cooling & Racks

Tier-3 Data Center Infrastructure, Server Rooms & Online UPS

Your core switches and enterprise servers are only as reliable as the room housing them. We build turnkey server rooms and micro-data centers featuring 42U/48U seismic cabinets, hot/cold aisle containment, precision in-row cooling, N+1 double-conversion online UPS banks, and FM-200 / Novec fire suppression integration.

In Simple Terms — Why It Matters

Grid power fluctuations, generator switchover gaps, and overheated server closets destroy expensive IT hardware. Our online UPS units deliver zero-millisecond battery transfer and send instant SMS alerts to your phone if room temperature or humidity ever spikes.
